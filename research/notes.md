# Research Notes: Bitnoir Web3 Product & UX

**Status:** researching
**Depth:** Deep

## Plan

- **Question:** Which proven web3 product and interface patterns should guide Bitnoir’s fair-launch site, member portal, and custody-sensitive flows?
- **Scope:** CoreDAO visual reference, portfolio and fair-launch information architecture, staking/rewards, referral systems, authentication, and manual USDT payment/withdrawal controls.
- **Audience:** Bitnoir product owner and implementation team.
- **Deliverable:** Evidence-backed recommendations applied to the Bitnoir redesign, with unresolved token economics identified rather than guessed.
