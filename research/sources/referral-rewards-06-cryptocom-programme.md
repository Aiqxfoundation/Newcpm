## **How does it work?**

1. Share your Referral Link or Referral Code with a friend

2. You and your friend will both receive rewards after they sign up using your link/code, pass KYC verification, and complete trading milestones within their first 30 days:


## **Trading Volume Milestones**

Both you and your friend earn rewards based on their total trading volume within 30 days of KYC approval:

Country Tier 1 (Switzerland, United States, Germany, Austria, Belgium, Luxembourg, Hong Kong, Finland, Cyprus):

- **$100** in trading volume: **$5 USD** each

- **$500** in trading volume: **$15 USD** each

- **$1,000** in trading volume: **$25 USD** each

- **$5,000** in trading volume: **$75 USD** each

- **Maximum per referral: $75 USD** each

## **March 2026 Referral Leaderboard Campaign**

We are introducing a monthly competition where top referrers can win a share of a **$30,000 USD** prize pool in CRO. This campaign runs in addition to the standard referral rewards, allowing you to earn extra bonuses by competing against other referrers globally.

**When does the campaign run?**

- **Launch Date:** 2nd March 2026

- **Campaign Period:** 2nd March 2026 – 31st March 2026

- **Frequency:** A new referral campaign will be launched each month.


**What are the rewards?** The total prize pool is **$25,000 USD in CRO**, split into two categories:

1. **Leaderboard Rewards ($25,000 USD total):** The top 200 referrers ranked by trading volume will share this pool based on their rank.





   - **Rank 1:** $5,000 USD

   - **Rank 2–5:** $1,000 USD each

   - **Rank 6–20:** $500 USD each

   - **Rank 21–100:** $75 USD each

   - **Rank 101–200:** $25 USD each


**How do I participate and qualify?** To appear on the leaderboard and be eligible for rewards, you must meet the following criteria during the campaign period:

- **Referral Count:** You must refer at least **3 friends** within the campaign period.

- **Trading Volume:** Your referees must generate a cumulative total of at least **$1,000 USD** in trading volume.


**How is rank determined?** Ranking is primarily based on the highest **Referral Trading Volume**. If two users have the same volume, the user with the higher **Number of Friends Referred** will rank higher.

- _Note: In the rare event of a tie in both volume and referral count, tied participants will receive the reward for the higher position (e.g., if two users tie for rank #100, both receive the Rank 100 reward)._


**Where can I see my ranking?** You can view your real-time rank and progress on the **Leaderboard** tab.

- Go to the "Referral Bonus" screen in the app.

- Tap the **Leaderboard** tab (located next to "Refer" and "Claim").

- The leaderboard displays the top 200 users, your current rank, and the trading volume required to reach the next tier.


**When will the rewards be disbursed?** Rewards for the leaderboard will be disbursed to your CDC app wallet within 7 days of campaign end.

## **How do my referees sign up using my Referral Code?**

## **Option 1: Sign up using a Referral Link**

1. Tap the Referral Link

2. Download the Crypto.com App from the App Store or Google Play

3. Confirm the referral code and submit it

4. Create a new account by submitting your email and confirming it

5. Complete KYC verification process by submitting a phone number, confirming it, and submitting relevant identification documents

6. Once you have been verified, start trading to earn rewards


## **Option 2: Sign up using a Referral Code**

1. Download the Crypto.com App from the App Store or Google Play

2. Tap “ **Have a Referral Code?”** button on the app landing page

3. Paste the Referral Code

4. Alternatively, you can also enter your referral code on the email signup page.

5. Create a new account by submitting your email and confirming it

6. Complete KYC verification process by submitting a phone number, confirming it, and submitting an identification document

7. Once you have been verified, start trading to earn referral rewards

## **Important Notes**

## **Eligibility Requirements**

1. Users must not have previously enrolled in the referral programme or other promotional programme. Only users who use the referral code to create an account, pass KYC verification, and complete trading within the desired period are qualified for the programme.

2. The referee must be from regions eligible for the referral programme and follow the rules designed for their region. If any related product or service is unavailable in your region, you will not be able to participate. Rewards may also vary based on the referee's jurisdiction.

3. You must have an active account to be eligible for any bonus.

4. For all users, excluding those in Europe, both the phone country and the submitted identity document must be from the same country to be eligible for the referral program.


## **30-Day Window**

1. The 30-day reward period begins at the moment of KYC approval (not signup initiation).

2. The window ends exactly 30 days later at the same time. For example: KYC approved January 1, 11:00 PM → Window ends January 31, 11:00 PM.

3. All trades completed within this 30-day window count toward the trading volume milestones.

4. Rewards are credited directly to both the referrer and referee's wallets within 24 hours after completing any trading milestone. For the Level Up milestone, please note that you will only get your $25 reward after 30 days of subscribing to the Level Up annual plan. The reward for card staking will be disbursed within 24 hours for the Level Up program.


## **Trading Volume Calculation**

1. Trading volume includes both buy and sell transactions.

2. Volume is calculated in USD equivalent at the time of trade.

3. Eligible transactions include:





   - Payment methods: Crypto, credit card (including Apple Pay, Google Pay), fiat

   - Transaction types: One-time, recurring, target order (after execution)

   - Asset types: Crypto (stocks and stablecoins are not included)


4. Trading volume is cumulative and calculated as the sum of all qualifying transactions during the 30-day period.

5. Milestones are tiered, meaning rewards are earned when each milestone is reached. For example, reaching $500 in trading volume earns you both the $100 milestone reward and the $500 milestone reward.


## **Reward Cap**

1. Users can earn rewards for up to **100 successful referrals**, after which there will be no further rewards granted.

2. A referral is considered successful when the referee completes at least one trading milestone after KYC verification.

3. The maximum amount each referrer can earn from referral rewards across all referrals is **$10,000 USD** (100 referrals × $100 maximum per referral for T1 countries).


## **Additional Terms**

1. We reserve the right to determine the relevant USD:CRO exchange rate for all bonuses paid out. The equivalent USD:CRO exchange rate is calculated based on the exchange rate at the time rewards are credited to your wallet.

2. Crypto.com Prime referral rewards has been terminated until further notice.

3. New users who register a referral code during account creation will not be eligible for the Welcome Bonus Program, while those who did not register with a referral code will automatically be eligible for the Welcome Bonus Program. However, users can still switch to the Referral Bonus Program program by navigating to the referral dashboard and tapping **‘Add a referral code’,** provided it iswithin 14 days of their KYC approval and they have not yet made a first trade.

4. New users who signed up with a referral code between 3rd November 2025 to 3rd December 2025 may be excluded from the referral program if they are also enrolled in the new user welcome bonus program, as the referral program is mutually exclusive from the new user welcome bonus program.

5. Crypto.com retains the right to nullify any referral or referral campaign rewards if fraud, manipulation, or suspicious activity is detected.

6. For Australian users, both the phone country and submitted identity documents must be from Australia for users to participate in the referral program.

7. For full T&Cs to the giveaway, please refer to the terms and conditions outlined here. [https://crypto.com/document/app-giveaway-campaign-sep-2025-5k](https://crypto.com/document/app-giveaway-campaign-sep-2025-5k)

## **How do I track my progress?**

## **For Referees (within 30 days of signup):**

Your referral page will display:

- A countdown timer showing time remaining in your 30-day window

- Your current trading volume and progress toward the next milestone

- A checklist of completed and pending milestones

- Your total earnings so far


## **For Referrers:**

Your referral dashboard will display:

- Your total referral earnings

- The number of successful referrals

- Individual referee progress (with masked names for privacy)

- Milestone completion status for each referee

- Real-time updates after each transaction


Progress tracking is updated in near real-time.
